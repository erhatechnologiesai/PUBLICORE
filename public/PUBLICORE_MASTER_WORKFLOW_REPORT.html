2. Complete End-to-End System Workflow

This section details how the PUBLICORE publishing pipeline operates from initial researcher manuscript submission to live peer-reviewed dissemination with registered Crossref DOIs.

Stage 1: Scholar Authentication & Single Sign-On (SSO) Route: /login

Researchers can sign in via email/password or authenticate directly via their 16-digit persistent ORCID iD (e.g., 0000-0002-1825-0097). The system issues a cryptographically signed session token and sets an encrypted HttpOnly cookie (publicore_session). The Next.js Edge Middleware intercepts all subsequent dashboard requests to enforce strict 5-tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Author, Reviewer, Editor, Production, and Admin.

Stage 2: 8-Step Manuscript Intake & AI Metadata Extraction Route: /submit

The author enters the responsive submission wizard. Upon uploading the research manuscript (PDF/DOCX), the AI intake parser automatically extracts title, structured abstract, keyword taxonomy, and co-author affiliations. The state machine assigns an official tracking code (e.g., PUB-2026-X89), creates the sequential lifecycle timeline, and transitions status to Submitted.

Stage 3: Editorial Desk & Automated AI Pre-Screening Route: /dashboard/editor/screening/[id]

The Chief Editor receives the manuscript in the triage queue. The AI Pre-Screening Engine (/api/ai/screen) analyzes the abstract to calculate three quantitative indices: Scope Alignment (0-100), Methodological Rigor (0-100), and Novelty Factor (0-100). High-performing manuscripts advance to peer review; predatory or out-of-scope papers receive immediate desk rejection with automated diagnostic feedback.

Stage 4: Reviewer Matchmaking & Conflict-of-Interest (COI) Filtering Route: /dashboard/editor/assign-reviewers/[id]

The editor accesses the matchmaking engine (/api/ai/reviewer-match), which scans the institutional registry and publication history. The system computes keyword overlap and verifies institutional distance to guarantee zero institutional conflicts of interest. The editor selects 2 to 3 peer experts and dispatches electronic invitations.

Stage 5: Double-Blind Peer Review Workspace & 4-Point Scoring Route: /dashboard/reviewer/workspace/[id]

Assigned reviewers evaluate the anonymized paper within a dedicated double-blind workspace. Reviewers assess the paper against a 4-point scientific rubric: Originality, Methodology, Clarity, and Significance. Reviewers submit two separate comment streams: public constructive remarks for authors and confidential deliberations visible only to the editorial board.

Stage 6: Editorial Adjudication & Author Revision Desk Route: /dashboard/editor/decision/[id]

The Editor evaluates compiled scorecards and issues a formal decision: Accept, Minor Revision, Major Revision, or Reject. If revision is requested, the author enters the Revision Desk (/dashboard/author/revisions/[id]) to upload point-by-point rebuttal responses and revised files, advancing the manuscript version number sequentially.

Stage 7: Production Desk, ANSI/NISO JATS XML & DOI Minting Route: /dashboard/production

Upon acceptance, the manuscript enters the production typesetting desk. The production engine serializes compliant ANSI/NISO Z39.96-2021 (JATS XML 1.3) containing complete metadata (ISSN, DOI, author ORCIDs, structured abstract). With 1-click, the specialist triggers "Publish & Mint DOI", registering the Crossref DOI (e.g., 10.1042/pub.2026.xxxx) and publishing the article live.

Stage 8: Live Open Access Dissemination & Firestore Cloud Sync Route: /articles/[id]

The published article is immediately indexed in the open access reader with multi-theme switcher (Light/Dark/Sepia), instant citation exporter (BibTeX, RIS, APA, IEEE), zoomable figures, and live Altmetric counters. Simultaneously, the document is synchronized to Google Cloud Firestore (publicore-24c29) with an immutable security audit trail log.
